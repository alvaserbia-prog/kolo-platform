# Rulebook on the Participation of Children

*This Rulebook governs the participation of minors in the KOLO system — accession, the scope of activities, the relationship with the parent, the contribution from the children's space, and the transition to an adult account. It is adopted on the basis of Article 58 of the KOLO System Rulebook (v4.4.1), which expressly refers to this act. In the event of inconsistency, the provisions of the KOLO System Rulebook shall prevail.*

## I — General Provisions

### Article 1

This Rulebook governs the participation of minors in the KOLO system, in accordance with Article 58 of the KOLO System Rulebook.

### Article 2

A minor user is a person who has reached seven and has not reached eighteen years of age.

### Article 3

A minor user participates with the consent of a parent or legal guardian (hereinafter: the parent).

The legal basis for processing the data of a minor user under fifteen years of age is parental consent, pursuant to Article 16 of the Law on Personal Data Protection. For a minor user who has reached fifteen years of age, parental consent is the legal basis for their participation as a contracting party.

The system does not verify age. Verification is not necessary because a minor user has no reason to declare their age untruthfully: an unverified adult account receives nothing until someone confirms it in the chain of confirmations, so a false declaration of majority would lead to an empty account, whereas a declaration that the person is a child leads to friendships and to the recording of the contribution under Article 14b.

## II — Accession

### Article 4 — Entry via a Parental Account

A minor user's account is opened by the parent from their own account. An account opened in this way is immediately in the "active" state under Article 4c.

Before any data about the child is entered, the parent is shown a notice about the processing of data and about their powers and responsibilities under this Rulebook.

The number of children per parent is not limited.

### Article 4a — Independent Accession by a Minor

A minor may open an account independently. Upon opening, they enter a pseudonym, a password, and the parent's email address.

The date of birth is not entered upon opening. It is entered by the parent upon taking over the account (Article 7).

Until the account is taken over, only the pseudonym and the parent's email address are processed about the minor user.

The parent's email address is data about a third party entered by the minor. It is processed on the basis of legitimate interest, for the purpose of obtaining the consent required by law, and solely in order to send the message under Article 4b.

### Article 4b — Invitation to the Parent and Takeover of the Account

A message with three actions is sent to the address under Article 4a: takeover of the account, a declaration that the person is not that child's parent, and deletion of the account.

The message states only the minor user's pseudonym. The name and other data are not stated, because it is not known whether the address is correct.

The message contains a one-time authorization with a period of seven days.

A declaration that the person is not that child's parent extinguishes the invitation; the account is not deleted thereby. Deletion of the account under this provision is executed without login, on the basis of the one-time authorization under paragraph 3.

An account that nobody takes over within fourteen days of opening is deleted. The period relates exclusively to the takeover of the account.

The parent takes over the account in two ways: via the email address stated by the minor, or by entering the minor user's pseudonym and the six-digit code visible in their profile. The second parent takes over the account by the same path. A minor user may have at most two parents; both have identical powers under this Rulebook. The minor user's consent to the establishment of parenthood is not required.

### Article 4c — Account States

A minor user's account is in one of three states:

1) **pending** — the account exists, the link with a parent has not been established. The minor user has a profile and makes friendships under Article 14a. They have no access to the Chat room and do not publish listings, do not participate in the communication under Article 12, and do not participate in the updating of the POEN record;

2) **linked** — the parent has taken over the account, but is not a verified user within the meaning of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. The Chat room, listings, and communication are available. The contribution under Article 14b is not recorded;

3) **active** — at least one parent is a verified user. Full access within the limits of this Rulebook.

The limitations under paragraph 1, item 1 are of a security nature and are explained in Article 18, paragraph 2.

The account moves between states by itself, according to the parent's status. The transition from the "active" state to the "linked" state is not a measure and the periods prescribed for suspension do not run.

### Article 5

A parent who opens an account under Article 4 must have a reality index of at least ten per cent.

The number of persons who have confirmed their reality is not a condition. An initial user satisfies the condition under paragraph 1 even where no person has confirmed them, because they are the origin of the chain of confirmations.

For the takeover of an account under Article 4b, the reality index is not a condition. Upon takeover the account moves to the "linked" state; it moves to the "active" state when the parent acquires the status of a verified user.

### Article 6

Upon the opening of an account under Article 4, the persons who have confirmed the parent's reality are invited to confirm that the parent has a child of the stated age. Confirmation is given for each child separately.

The period for making a declaration is thirty days from the opening of the account.

A person who does not make a declaration within that period has their confirmation of that parent's reality annulled. The annulment withdraws the POEN records recorded on account of that confirmation and releases that person's verification slot.

A declaration made after the expiry of the period does not remove the annulment that has taken effect.

An annulled confirmation may be repeated, and the parent's reality may also be confirmed by another person, under the general rules. With each new confirmation of the parent's reality, the procedure under this Article is carried out anew.

A declaration disputing the existence of the child requires a statement of reasons; it is decided upon by the Management Board.

### Article 7

The age of a minor user is established from the date of birth stated by the parent — upon the opening of the account (Article 4) or upon its takeover (Article 4b). The stated date is not changed.

A minor user may themselves state the school they attend, by choosing from the list of schools. Stating a school is not a condition for using the account.

### Article 7a — The Minor User's E-mail Address

A minor user may state their own e-mail address. Stating an address is voluntary and is not a condition for using the account.

The address referred to in paragraph 1 is processed exclusively for the purpose of regaining access to the account — setting a new password when the minor user has forgotten it. No notifications or other mail are sent to that address.

The address is recorded on the account only after the minor user has confirmed it by opening a link sent to that very address. Until confirmation the address has no effect and is not recorded on the account. The confirmation link is valid for twenty-four hours.

The minor user removes the stated address at any time.

Upon transition to an adult account the stated address is retained, since an adult account has an e-mail address in any event. The data is erased upon cessation of the capacity of user.

The parent's e-mail address referred to in Article 4a does not become the address of the minor user's account and is not affected by this Article.

## III — The Relationship Between the Parent and the Minor User

### Article 8

The parent gives consent once, upon the opening or takeover of the account. The parent does not approve individual actions of the minor user.

### Article 9 — Parental Insight

The parent opens their child's profile from their own account. The profile contains the records of POEN record updates and published listings.

The parent does not read the conversations that the minor user has with other minor users. Instead of content, the parent has access to the list of friendships with dates and the list of conversations, without messages. The reason is that insight into a conversation also touches the other child, whose parent has not granted that insight.

A minor user's conversation with an adult user is an exception: the parent reads it. The parent does not write in that conversation. The adult user is shown a notice in the conversation that the conversation is read by a parent.

The parent is notified of a conversation under paragraph 3 upon the first message in that conversation.

The minor user is informed of the scope of the insight under this Article.

### Article 10

On their child's profile, the parent may remove any listing the child has published.

At the same place, the parent approves, by means of a switch, the minor user's communication and exchange with adult users. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

On their child's profile the parent sets a new password for the minor user. The previous password is not required for this, since the parent does not know it. The minor user is notified of the change. This power exists because a minor user's account as a rule has no e-mail address, so the forgotten-password procedure is not available to them.

The parent is liable for content published by the minor user up to the moment of removal, and for the actions of the minor user on the Platform.

The parent does not end their child's friendships (Article 14c, paragraph 1).

### Article 11

The parent has no account in the children's space, does not publish content in it, and does not address any minor user.

## IV — Scope of Activities

### Article 12

Minor users constitute a single group, without classification by age. This provision is transitional: classification into age groups, with its own rules of adjacency, is introduced by a decision of the Management Board, by amendment of this Rulebook.

If classification is introduced, it applies only to friendships made after its introduction; it does not end existing friendships and does not lead to the write-off of the contribution under Article 14b.

A minor user communicates and exchanges with other minor users.

Communication and exchange with adult users are permitted with the parent's consent under Article 10.

An account in the "pending" state does not participate in the communication under this Article.

### Article 13

The same conditions apply to the publication of a minor user's listing as to the listing of any other user. Only visibility differs.

A minor user's listing is visible to minor users and to their parents. The listing is not publicly available and is not displayed in search engines.

With the parent's consent under Article 10, the listing is also visible to adult users.

### Article 14 — Updating the POEN Record

The channels for recording the contribution to platform content and the exchange contribution under the KOLO System Rulebook do not apply to minor users.

A parent and their child re-register POEN to one another without any limit on the amount, in both directions.

Minor users re-register POEN among themselves.

The re-registration of POEN between a minor and an adult user is permitted with the parent's consent under Article 10.

For a minor user there is no monthly limit, no prohibition period, and no annulment of a re-registration; the history is visible as it is for adult accounts.

A minor user's record may be negative exclusively under Article 14c, paragraph 3 and Article 19, paragraph 2. This is the third exception to the prohibition of a negative record under Article 14, paragraph 3 of the KOLO System Rulebook, alongside restitution under Article 20b of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality and the annulment of a record update following an exchange report. The restitution regime applies to such a record: it is not a debt, it is not collected, POEN received first replenishes it, re-registration to another is possible only once it passes zero, and the exchange of goods and services is not restricted.

### Article 14a — Friendships

Minor users make a friendship exclusively by scanning a one-time code, directly, one in the presence of the other. The code is valid for five minutes and does not contain a pronounceable number.

The number of friendships per minor user is not limited.

The parent does not approve a friendship. The parent is notified of a friendship that has been made and sees the list of friendships with dates.

### Article 14b — The Minor User's Contribution

For a friendship that has been made, the Protocol records 500 POEN to each minor user.

The contribution is recorded when both parties are in the "active" state under Article 4c. While one party is waiting, the contribution is noted and visible to both parties, but it is not a POEN record and does not enter the balance, the circulation, or public aggregates.

The channel is an automatic act of the Protocol and does not count towards the daily limit of Protocol Programs. It is the channel under item 9 of Article 15 of the KOLO System Rulebook.

A friendship between minor users who have a parent in common is made, but carries no contribution.

Recording under this Article is the only channel by which new POEN records arise in the children's space. The POEN thereby recorded enters the total number of recorded POEN and thus the accounting framework of the system — it shifts the threshold of the founding step and the accounting coefficient of ZRNO, in both directions, because it is written off under Articles 14c and 19.

### Article 14c — Ending a Friendship

A friendship is ended by a minor user, either of the two parties. The parent does not end a friendship.

Upon the ending, 500 POEN is written off from both parties, provided the contribution under Article 14b had been recorded.

The write-off is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative (Article 14, paragraph 6). Without this, a minor user who immediately re-registers the POEN would avoid the write-off, and the cycle of making and ending the same friendship would be a source of unlimited recording.

Before the ending, the minor user is shown a notice about the write-off.

An ended friendship may be made again; the contribution is then recorded anew.

### Article 15

A minor user does not inscribe ZRNO and does not participate in decision-making in the Upper Kolo. A minor user has no reality index and confirms no one. These limitations are not removed by parental consent.

### Article 15a — The School and the Overview by School

The school referred to in Article 7 is chosen by the minor user themselves, from the list of schools; free-text entry is not possible. The stated school is displayed on the minor user's profile.

The Platform publishes an overview by school: the number of minor users having at least one parent who is a full member, and their share in the number of pupils enrolled at that school. This overview contains no personal data and is available to everyone.

An overview of the minor users of a single school, with their pseudonym and the balance of their POEN record, is available to signed-in users. That overview does not give access to a minor user's profile (Article 15b).

The stated school may be changed at most once every thirty days. The school datum is deleted upon transition to an adult account and upon termination of the capacity of user; no history of earlier choices is kept.

A position in the overview referred to in this Article is not a ground for recording a contribution and carries no POEN.

### Article 15b — Access to the Profile of a Minor User

The profile of a minor user is not available to adult users. Instead of the profile, a notice is displayed containing the pseudonym of the minor user and the pseudonym of their parent.

The parental consent referred to in Article 10 does not open access to the profile; it governs communication and exchange.

Contact between an adult user and a minor user is established in connection with a listing published by the minor user, in the manner set out in Article 12 and subject to the parental insight under Article 9.

A minor user may access the profile of a minor user with whom they have formed a friendship.

Information on who the parents of a minor user are, and on who the children of an adult user are, is available to signed-in users.

This Article does not restrict the Foundation in acting upon a report or in removing content.

## V — The Chat Room

### Article 18

Minor users have a single Chat room, separate from the Chat room of adult users.

The Chat room is not available to an account in the "pending" state. The reason is a security one: behind an account introduced by a parent stands a verified adult user and the persons who confirmed them, whereas behind a pending account stands no one. Until the account is taken over, that check does not exist even indirectly, because the parent does not read conversations between children (Article 9, paragraph 2).

In the Chat room, a minor user sees the messages of their friends under Article 14a.

A reply quoting another's message is not supported, because it would circumvent the limitation under paragraph 3.

## VI — Majority, Dormancy, and Termination

### Article 19 — Transition to an Adult Account

Upon reaching eighteen years of age, the account ceases to be a minor user's account.

On that day the POEN recorded under Article 14b is annulled: for the user, in the amount of the product of the number of valid recorded friendships and 500 POEN, and for each of those friends, 500 POEN. The annulment is executed even where the record thereby becomes negative, under the restitution regime of Article 14, paragraph 6. Contributions relating to ended friendships, to friendships under Article 14b, paragraph 4, and to friendships that had not been recorded are not annulled.

The user's parents confirm their reality — each one confirmation, under the general rules of the Rulebook on Proof of Reality. A confirmation that those rules do not permit is not recorded.

The friendships under Article 14a cease.

Other POEN records are not touched.

The user and their friends are notified of the consequences under paragraph 2 one month in advance.

Rationale: a friendship under Article 14b arises from a direct meeting and carries 500 POEN, whereas a confirmation of reality carries 1,000 POEN but requires a statement about another person's identity and one's own liability for its truthfulness. Without the annulment, a user who accedes immediately before majority would retain a stock from the cheaper channel and acquire the more expensive one alongside it, thereby being permanently in a more favourable position than a peer who accedes one month later.

### Article 20 — Dormancy

Where the parent's reality is no longer confirmed to the extent prescribed by the Rulebook on Proof of Reality, the minor user's account moves to the "linked" state under Article 4c. No data are deleted.

The parent's own account is not affected thereby.

### Article 21

A parent may delete their child's account.

Deletion of the account annuls all of the minor user's POEN records, with a corresponding counter-entry of the Protocol, and removes all of their listings. Friendships cease without a write-off on the side of other minor users, because the POEN has already been annulled in full.

## VII — Final Provisions

### Article 22

The module is activated upon the adoption of this Rulebook, the alignment of the KOLO System Rulebook, the Terms of Use, and the Privacy Policy, and upon the update of the data protection impact assessment.

### Article 23

This Rulebook enters into force on the day of its adoption.
